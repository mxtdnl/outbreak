Situation Briefing

A novel pathogen has been confirmed in Meridian City (population 500,000). You are the Emergency Public Health Director. Over 12 simulated weeks you will make weekly resource and policy decisions within a hard weekly budget. All decisions bind.

The population is split into a general group and a smaller clinically vulnerable group with far higher fatality risk — how you protect each shapes both deaths and public life. Events will interrupt you; some can be blunted by early investment. Choose your scenario and difficulty below.

Model Notes

Two-group SEIR with waning immunity; vaccinated people are tracked separately and lose immunity more slowly than recovered people. Vulnerable group has far higher fatality. Overflow sharply raises mortality.


Variants raise R₀ for the rest of the game and can stack, up to a ceiling of 1.45 × the starting R₀. Forecasts are model estimates: lower modelling levels make larger errors.


Rₑ includes immunity: above 1 means infections are growing. The economy recovers +1.2/wk only with no lockdown, school closure or travel restriction, and at most voluntary shielding.


Compliance (from comms + financial support) scales how effective restrictions really are. Unspent budget does not carry forward.


Debrief composite: 40% deaths · 30% economy · 30% satisfaction. The Class Scorecard after the debrief scores each lobby separately and the government on the geometric mean of all of them.
